# Risks & Constraints

## Context
Older shoppers may struggle with small text, too many buttons, and confusing checkout steps.

## Content
- Too many options: remove buttons and information we do not need.
- Small text: use large text and buttons with clear colours.
- Hard-to-find checkout: make the checkout button bigger and clearer.
- Confusing steps: show what to do next.
- Unclear order status: show a clear confirmation message.
- Limited time: focus on the main features first.

The Lab 2 tester understood the product pictures and prices, but struggled to find checkout.

## Decisions
- Keep the clear product information and improve checkout - Caique, October 20, 2026.
